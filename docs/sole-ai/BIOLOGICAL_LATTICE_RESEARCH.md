# Biological lattice research: where, what, and why

This note records the research basis and safety constraints for the biological
sole lattice generated by `generate-envelope-lattice`. It is a geometry design
candidate, not proof that the sole can safely carry a runner. Coupon fatigue,
compression, printability, and shoe-level validation remain mandatory.

## Primary research used

- Martínez, Song, Dumas, and Lefebvre, *Orthotropic k-nearest Foams for
  Additive Manufacturing* (ACM TOG, 2017),
  <https://doi.org/10.1145/3072959.3073638>. The paper supports stochastic,
  non-periodic k-nearest foam synthesis with freely oriented anisotropy and
  smooth spatial grading.
- Martínez, Dumas, and Lefebvre, *Procedural Voronoi Foams for Additive
  Manufacturing* (ACM TOG, 2016),
  <https://doi.org/10.1145/2897824.2925922>. It motivates aperiodic open-cell
  foam, local density control, and implicit evaluation for fabrication.
- Runions, Lane, and Prusinkiewicz, *Modeling Trees with a Space Colonization
  Algorithm* (Eurographics, 2007),
  <https://doi.org/10.2312/NPH/NPH07/063-070>. It provides the attraction-point
  growth model used to make branches divide and converge organically.
- Tero et al., *Rules for biologically inspired adaptive network design*
  (Science, 2010), <https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1177894>. It motivates
  anastomosis: redundant loop connections that balance path cost and fault
  tolerance rather than leaving a fragile tree.
- Kumar et al., *Inverse-designed spinodoid metamaterials* (npj Computational
  Materials, 2020), <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-020-0341-6>. It supports
  non-periodic, smoothly graded, directionally biased, bone-like cellular
  materials without a repeated unit cell.
- Wu, Aage, Westermann, and Sigmund, *Infill Optimization for Additive
  Manufacturing—Approaching Bone-Like Porous Structures* (IEEE TVCG, 2018),
  <https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2017.2655523>. It supports locally constrained
  porous infill as a route to sparse but stable bone-like structures.

## Implemented adaptation

The current generator is a conservative hybrid rather than a verbatim copy of
one paper:

1. Each left/right source STL is rasterized independently. Separated vertical
   solids remain separate; neither foot is mirrored from the other.
2. Stochastic attraction points are sampled independently per build layer.
   V10 targets 50% of the V8 plan-view node density. Node targets are multiplied
   by 0.50, while spacing and coverage radii scale by `1/sqrt(0.50)` so coverage
   repair does not silently restore the old density.
3. Each attraction point connects to two or three valid k-nearest parents in
   the preceding layer. A parent without a child receives a local rescue
   attractor, so no internal free tip is accepted.
4. Multiple parents may reach one attraction and one parent may feed multiple
   attractions. These merge/split paths provide the anastomosis and irregular
   openings associated with living transport networks.
5. Quadratic curved paths are used when every sampled point remains printable
   and inside the source. A straight chord is the conservative fallback.
6. Radius follows a smooth load field and is clamped to 0.75--1.00 mm, giving
   an exact free-branch diameter range of 1.5--2.0 mm.
7. An implicit smooth union rounds every meeting point. Junctions can become
   locally wider where several legal branches merge; individual free shafts do
   not exceed 2.0 mm.
8. The sparse 2.0 mm branches prevent a second legal parent path near a few
   narrow boundaries. V10 permits at most 9% single-parent nodes; measured
   production-grid values are 8.15% left and 8.83% right. Average parent links
   must still exceed 2.2, and internal dead ends remain forbidden.

## Boundary contract

- Bottom and top are the only anchor surfaces. A 1.2 mm conformal anchor layer
  joins the lattice to the outsole and upper-sole interfaces.
- No lateral shell or lateral anchor is generated.
- Every branch surface preserves a measured 0.25 mm side margin. The recorded
  centreline margin varies with the local 0.75--1.00 mm branch radius; a branch
  whose actual surface enters that margin is rejected before STL export.
- Thin source intervals at or below 6 mm remain solid because a reliable
  branch network cannot be fitted there without damaging the supplied shape.
- The final mesh must be watertight, have no side-clipped branches or internal
  dead ends, and pass coverage and parent-link gates before it is written.

## Remaining evidence gap

The literature justifies the topology family, not this exact TPU shoe. The
load field is still heuristic because measured plantar pressure, TPU material
curves, FEA, and cyclic-compression results are not yet available. The V10
density-50 STL must therefore be treated as a review and coupon candidate, not
print-ready running equipment.
